Print tip: Output is prepared at 300 DPI. In your printer’s Properties/Preferences, choose the matching paper type and High/Best quality. In the browser print dialog, select A4, Portrait, 100% / Actual size, and disable headers and footers. To open full Windows printer properties, close the browser print window and press Ctrl+Shift+P on the print page (or choose Print using system dialog). Then select your Canon G2010 → Preferences/Properties → Glossy Photo Paper → High/Best. The app cannot force driver settings automatically.
